## About NoScrubs

NoScrubs is an on-demand laundry pickup and delivery service. Customers schedule a pickup
through the NoScrubs app or website. Laundry is collected from the customer's doorstep,
professionally washed, dried, and neatly folded, then returned the same day—in as little
as 4 hours.

Pickups are completed within a 2-hour pickup window by trained laundry professionals,
known as Scrubbers.

NoScrubs uses hypoallergenic, unscented detergents and follows strict hygiene standards.
Special care is taken to follow any customer-provided instructions for handling laundry.
